Frequently Asked Questions

Where is The Pinnacle located?

On Road No. 2 of the Financial District, Nanakramguda (Kokapet), Hyderabad 500032 — along the Outer Ring Road, 4–9 minutes from the Financial District office corridor.

What are the offered unit types?

3.5 BHK sky residences of 3,587–3,678 sq ft in Tower A and 4.5 BHK sky residences of 4,545–4,622 sq ft in Tower B. Each 55-storey tower holds just four residences per floor.

What is the price range?

3.5 BHK residences range from ₹5.25 to ₹6 Cr; 4.5 BHK residences from ₹6.5 to ₹8 Cr. Prices are indicative — enquire for the current price sheet and availability.

What is the status of the project?

Both towers are under construction (site photographs above are from February 2026), with possession expected in June 2028.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes — Telangana RERA No. P02400006797, Building Permit 001689/BP/HMDA/0359/SKP/2023. Details can be verified at rera.telangana.gov.in.

What amenities does the community offer?

A clubhouse with infinity pool, spa, gym, mini theatre, sports zone, party hall, café, lounge and guest suites; coworking, study, hobby, toddler and elders' spaces within the towers; and courts, gardens, an amphitheatre and jogging track across the 79% open podium.
